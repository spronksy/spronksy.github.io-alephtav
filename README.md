# spronksy.github.io/alephtav

Aleph Tav · אֵת

A free, open-access explorer of the Hebrew alphabet as a living system of transformation.

What this is:

The Hebrew letters are not merely symbols for sound. In Kabbalistic tradition, each letter is a transformation operator, a specific movement of energy from potential to form, from the hidden to the revealed, from source to expression.
Aleph Tav makes this knowledge freely accessible to anyone. It covers all 22 letters of the Hebrew alphabet plus the 5 final forms (sofit letters), 27 in total, with genuine depth at every level.

What each letter includes:

The letter and its name - Hebrew character, transliteration, numerical value

Group and elemental association - Mother, Double, or Simple letter; elemental and astrological correspondence

Milui — the inner spelling of the letter's name, broken into its component letters, each with its own meaning
Transformation operators - what the Milui sequence is actually doing as a movement of energy; the specific arc from one state to another that the spelling encodes

Kabbalistic associations - connections to the Sefirot, to Torah, to Zoharic and Talmudic teaching, to number symbolism and cosmology

How to apply it - practical guidance for working with the letter's energy in your life, work, relationships, and creative process

In Torah - the letter's first or most significant appearance in the sacred text

Reflection question - a single question to sit with


The Name:

Aleph Tav (אֵת) is the untranslatable Hebrew particle that appears throughout Torah before direct objects, invisible in most translations, yet present everywhere. Kabbalists read it as the signature of the whole alphabet: from Aleph (the first letter) to Tav (the last), the entirety of creation implied in two letters. Every time אֵת appears in Torah, the whole alphabet and therefore the whole of creation is silently invoked.
It is the name that names everything without naming any one thing.

The Ethic:

Foundational knowledge belongs to everyone.

The teachings encoded in the Hebrew letters are ancient, universal, and not owned by any person or institution. This explorer exists to make that knowledge accessible, clearly, deeply, and without gatekeeping.

It is free. It will remain free.

Sustaining this work is another matter. If tools, frameworks, or guided resources emerge from this body of work, things that take years of study, synthesis, and care to build, it is not only acceptable but necessary to offer those for exchange. The knowledge is not for sale. The map drawn through it, the structure built around it, the presence that transmits it, those represent real labor, and real labor deserves to be sustained. Keeping the foundation free while building a livelihood on what grows from it is not a contradiction. It is integrity.

This includes creative works produced through new technologies. As ancient wisdom meets modern tools, new forms emerge: interactive experiences, AI-assisted frameworks, digital products, and applications that could not have existed before. These creative works, built on the foundation of sacred knowledge but shaped by human labor, ingenuity, and technological craft, are legitimate offerings. Charging for them honors both the work and the tradition it serves.

How to use it:

Open index.html in any browser, desktop or mobile. No installation, no account, no dependencies beyond an internet connection for font loading.

Live at: <https://spronksy.github.io/alephtav/>

Filter letters by group (Mother, Double, Simple, Final) or browse all 27. Tap any letter to open its full detail view. Use the reflection questions as entry points for journaling, meditation, or conversation.

Built with:

Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. One file. No framework, no build tools, no dependencies. Designed for mobile, works on everything.
Font: Cormorant Garamond + IM Fell English via Google Fonts.
